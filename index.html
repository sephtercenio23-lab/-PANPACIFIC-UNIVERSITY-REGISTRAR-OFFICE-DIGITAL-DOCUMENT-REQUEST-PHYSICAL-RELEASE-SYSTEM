Data Privacy Notice.

The Panpacific University Registrar's Office is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012). This digital evaluation form is designed to gather student feedback on our staff's performance, service efficiency, and professionalism. Your input will be used solely for quality assurance, training, and staff performance evaluation.

Your identifying details will only be accessible to authorized personnel from the Registrar's Office, and will never be shared with the specific staff member being evaluated.
